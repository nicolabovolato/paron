# Paron license FAQ

Paron uses the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE). This FAQ explains the license in plain
words. It is not the license. If this FAQ and the license are different, the license applies.

## Summary

You can use, change and share Paron for any purpose, with one exception: you cannot use Paron, or
its source code, to provide a product that competes with Paron, or with a product that the licensor
provides with Paron.

The license says:

> Any purpose is a permitted purpose, except for providing any product that competes with the
> software or any product the licensor or any of its affiliates provides using the software.

The license applies only to Paron and its source code. A product that has no part of Paron is not
under this license, and this license does not stop it.

## Why this license

Sharing is caring. The source of Paron is public, so you can see what runs on your machines.

But software is now much cheaper to build than it was. The hard part was always to get people to see
it. A person who is better at that can take a finished project, give it a new name and a new look,
and get more attention than the work below it. Paron is my idea and many hours of my work, and I do
not want that to occur to it.

This license is the middle way. The code is public, and you can use it for almost any purpose. Take
what you need. Do not take the product.

## Use of Paron

### Is Paron open source?

No. Paron is source-available. You can read, change and share the source, but the license has a
limit that an open source license does not have.

### Can I use Paron in my company?

Yes. You can use Paron to reach and manage your machines. The size of your company and the number of
machines are not important. The use is free.

### Can I use Paron for commercial work?

Yes. The license does not forbid commercial use. It forbids only a product that competes and that
you make with Paron or its source code.

### Can I use Paron at home?

Yes.

### Can I use Paron to manage machines that run the applications of my clients?

Yes, if only your personnel use Paron. Your clients use the applications. They do not use Paron. It
is not important who owns the machines.

### Can I install Paron for a client, for a fee?

Yes. The client then uses Paron for its own machines under this license.

### Can I give my clients accounts on a Paron that I operate?

Ask first. A service that gives other persons access to Paron can compete with Paron. Open an issue
at <https://github.com/nicolabovolato/paron/issues> and describe the service.

### Does the license apply to my machines and to the applications on them?

No. The license applies to Paron. It does not apply to the machines that you manage with Paron or to
the software on them.

## Changes and copies

### Can I change Paron?

Yes, for each permitted purpose. For example, you can add a function that your company needs.

### Can I fork the repository to prepare a contribution?

Yes.

### Can I share copies of Paron?

Yes. Each person who gets a copy from you must also get the license, or its address, and the line
that starts with `Required Notice:`.

### Can I put Paron in a package collection, an app catalogue or a container image?

Yes, if the copy is not changed and the license goes with it.

### Can I use the name "Paron" for my changed version?

The license gives you no right to the name or the logo. Give your changed version a different name.

## Products made with Paron or its source code

All the questions in this section are about a product that contains Paron, a changed Paron, or a
part of the Paron source code. If your product has no part of Paron, this section does not apply to
it.

### Can I use code from Paron in my own product?

Yes, if your product does not compete with Paron.

### What is a product that competes?

A product competes if a person can use it in place of Paron. The license says that a product
competes "even when provided free of charge", and that the interface, the platform and the
programming language make no difference.

These are examples of products that compete, if they contain Paron or a part of its source code:

- Paron with a different name, changed or not, for sale or free.
- Paron as a hosted or managed service for other persons.
- A product that uses the Paron agent, tunnel or terminal to give its users access to their
  machines.

### Can I make a product that competes from the Paron source code, and give it away free?

No. A free product also competes.

### Can I make a product that competes, with no code from Paron?

Yes. The license applies only to Paron and its source code.

### I made a product with Paron code. What if a later version of Paron competes with it?

If your product did not compete when you made it, you can continue with the versions of Paron from
before that time. You cannot use later versions of Paron in that product. The license says:

> you may continue using versions of the software available under these terms beforehand to provide
> your competing product, but not any later versions.

## Other questions

### What occurs if I do not obey the license?

The first time that you get a written notice, you have 32 days to obey the license and to correct
what you did. If you do that, your license continues. If you do not, your license ends.

Ask. Open an issue at <https://github.com/nicolabovolato/paron/issues>.
